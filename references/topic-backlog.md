## Status and maintenance

Use `draft`, `ready`, `scheduled`, `aired`, or `retired` only when a host has
established that status. Use `needs-review` for uncertain imported ideas.
Do not infer guest commitments, attendance, dates, or whether a topic aired.
After publication is confirmed, link the matching record in
[past-episodes.md](past-episodes.md). Drafting a rundown does not publish it.

An instruction to save something is not permission to expose private notes.
Before writing to this public file, confirm that the content is intended for
public release. Preserve uncertainty and provenance rather than silently
converting old suggestions into unused ideas or aired episodes.
