## Reading order

Use the live Apple episode listing first when readable, then check Spotify for
new releases or discrepancies when accessible. Either accessible live official
listing is preferable to assuming the bundled snapshot is current. Read at least
the latest 12 available episodes and their descriptions; compare candidate angles
against the wider accessible listing and local history. A 12-episode scan alone
does not establish that a subject has never been discussed.

For a suspected duplicate, open the individual episode page and inspect its
title, publication date, description, and identifier. Reuploads and platform
mirrors should not automatically count as new topics. Keep distinct releases
separate unless evidence supports merging them.

Prefer absolute publication dates from individual pages over rounded labels.
Recording/broadcast dates may differ from publication dates. Do not infer missing
years or assume an old description's host roster and schedule are current.
Titles and descriptions are publication metadata, not independent fact checks.
