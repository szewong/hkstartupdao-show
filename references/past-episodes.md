## Adding or correcting records

After publication, a host may explicitly request an archive update. Record the
final title, known publication or broadcast date (label which), public episode
URL/identifier, and discussion angle. Use `unknown` for missing details. Preserve
provenance and distinct upload IDs. Do not mark a proposed topic as aired or
publish private scheduling notes. Refreshing a local snapshot does not itself
commit changes to GitHub.
